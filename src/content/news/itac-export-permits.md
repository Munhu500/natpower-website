Natpower has renewed its export permits with South Africa's International Trade Administration Commission (ITAC), supporting the continued export of sulphuric acid from South Africa to customers in the region.

## Permits held

- ITAC Export Permit EXP2026/62348: renewal for export from South Africa to regional markets
- ITAC Export Permit EXP2026/58863: sulphuric acid and oleum
- ITAC Export Permit EXP2025/47633

## Compliance as standard

Sulphuric acid is classified as a dangerous good, and every stage of its movement, from export through to storage and delivery, is regulated. Natpower maintains the full set of permits and licences required on both sides of the border, so that customers can rely on uninterrupted, fully compliant supply.
