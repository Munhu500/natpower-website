Natpower Industrial Corporation Limited, the Zambian company in the Natpower Group, is pursuing a listing on the Lusaka Stock Exchange (LuSE).

## Growing with the region's mining sector

Since it began operating, the company has built a track record in supplying sulphuric acid, granular sulphur and silica sand to listed mining houses in Zambia. This is supported by specialised dangerous-goods logistics and a full set of regulatory permits and licences.

A listing on the LuSE is intended to support the next stage of that growth and to give investors the opportunity to participate in it.
