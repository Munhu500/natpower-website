Natpower Industrial Corporation Limited has renewed its ZAMRA import permit (WEB26/IPER/RW/0049) with an increased allocation.

## Supporting growing demand

The increased allocation allows Natpower to import larger volumes into Zambia, in line with growing demand from its mining customers.

## A complete compliance framework

The import permit sits alongside Natpower's Zambian licences from the Zambia Environmental Management Agency (ZEMA), which cover:

- sulphuric acid importation
- storage
- distribution
- pesticide and toxic substances

It also sits alongside South African export permits issued by ITAC.
