Natpower Industrial Corporation (Pty) Ltd, the South African company in the Natpower Group, has entered a toll-manufacturing partnership with a leading South African mining house.

## How the partnership works

Under the agreement, Natpower supplies elemental sulphur to the partner's acid production facilities. In return, Natpower receives a share of the sulphuric acid produced, which it supplies to mining customers across the region.

The model gives Natpower a dependable, South African-produced source of sulphuric acid, matched to the growing requirements of mining operations in Zambia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Malawi.

## Why it matters for the region

Sulphuric acid is essential to copper and cobalt production across the Central African Copperbelt, where it is used in leaching and processing. Reliable supply is critical: interruptions affect output at the mine.

By securing production at source, Natpower can offer its customers greater supply certainty, consistent quality and a single accountable partner from production through to delivery.

## Building on an established track record

The partnership builds on the operating experience of our Zambian company, Natpower Industrial Corporation Limited, which supplies sulphuric acid, granular sulphur and silica sand to listed mining houses in Zambia. It does so through a fleet of specialised bulk acid tankers and ZEMA-certified storage, backed by a full set of import, export, storage and distribution permits.

Together, the two sister companies of the Natpower Group now cover the full supply corridor, from production in South Africa to delivery at the mine.
